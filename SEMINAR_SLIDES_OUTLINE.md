## 🎯 Presentation Tips

### Before You Start
1. ✅ Test all links and demos
2. ✅ Have backup screenshots ready
3. ✅ Practice transitions
4. ✅ Time your presentation
5. ✅ Prepare for questions

### During Presentation
1. 🎤 Speak clearly and confidently
2. 👁️ Make eye contact
3. 🤚 Use hand gestures
4. ⏰ Watch the time
5. 😊 Smile and be enthusiastic

### Slide Transitions
- **Smooth:** Use animations sparingly
- **Timing:** 1-2 minutes per slide
- **Emphasis:** Pause on key points
- **Demo:** Take your time, show features

### Handling Questions
- **Listen:** Fully understand the question
- **Pause:** Think before answering
- **Clarify:** Restate if needed
- **Honest:** Say "I don't know" if true
- **Forward:** Offer to discuss later if complex
