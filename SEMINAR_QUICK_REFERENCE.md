## 🔍 Answer to Common Questions

**Q: Is it better than Google Forms?**
A: Yes! We auto-generate questions, have TTS, better analytics, work offline, and create PDF reports.

**Q: How accurate is scoring?**
A: MCQs are 100% accurate. Descriptive uses keyword matching (~75% accurate), upgradeable with NLP.

**Q: Can it prevent cheating?**
A: Yes - enforced timer, auto-submit, attempt tracking. Future: proctoring features.

**Q: Why Python/Flask?**
A: Fast development, rich libraries, easy deployment, readable code.

**Q: Production ready?**
A: Yes for 1-100 users. For scale, add database and caching.
